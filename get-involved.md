### Come on a trek or a camp

Our programmes are open to the general public and to students. Watch the
[Events](/events/) page for announcements and registration details.

### Become a member

Members take part in treks, climbs and camps through the year, and help run
them. Write to us at [thenaturelovers1986@gmail.com](mailto:thenaturelovers1986@gmail.com)
and tell us a little about yourself.

### Volunteer or support

Tree plantations, tank cleaning, children's camps and our 50th anniversary
exhibition in December 2026 all need hands. Donors and contributing members
keep our programmes affordable for students. To volunteer or to contribute,
please [write to us](mailto:thenaturelovers1986@gmail.com).
